Proximal Operators for Trust-Region Methods

Viscosity-based approximations in nonsmooth, regularized optimization
With Bobby Baraldi · Rapid Optimization Library team, Sandia National Laboratories · Summer 2026

Many optimization problems combine a smooth objective with a nonsmooth regularizer, such as an ℓ1 penalty that promotes sparsity. Trust-region methods for these problems repeatedly evaluate the proximal operator of the regularizer, which can be expensive or unavailable in closed form.

In my second summer at Sandia I developed proximal operator approximations for nonsmooth trust-region optimization based on viscosity solutions of Hamilton–Jacobi equations. The Moreau envelope of a function solves a Hamilton–Jacobi equation, and adding a small viscosity term gives a smooth approximation whose minimizer can be estimated from samples instead of solving a subproblem exactly.
