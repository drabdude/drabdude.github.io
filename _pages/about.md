I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Mathematics at the University of Washington, advised by [Heather Wilber](https://amath.washington.edu/people/heather-wilber). My research is on efficient algorithms for large-scale optimization and numerical linear algebra.

Most of my current work is on hierarchically semi-separable (HSS) matrices, where I develop fast solvers for linear systems and minimum-norm problems that take advantage of the low-rank structure of these matrices. With [Aleksandr Aravkin](https://amath.washington.edu/people/aleksandr-aravkin), I also work on Kalman smoothing and filtering for systems with non-Gaussian errors.

For the past two summers I have been a graduate research intern at Sandia National Laboratories, working with Bobby Baraldi on the Rapid Optimization Library team. There I have worked on trust-region methods for PDE-constrained and nonsmooth optimization, using mixed-precision linear algebra and approximate proximal operators to reduce the cost of each iteration.

Before coming to UW, I received a B.A. in Applied Mathematics and Mathematics from Rice University.
