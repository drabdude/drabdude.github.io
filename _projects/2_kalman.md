With [Aleksandr Aravkin](https://amath.washington.edu/people/aleksandr-aravkin), University of Washington, 2024 – present.

Classical Kalman filters and smoothers assume Gaussian process and measurement errors. I work on the development and theoretical analysis of a hybrid Kalman filtering/smoothing method for systems whose errors follow non-Gaussian distributions, aimed at real-time estimation in these nonstandard regimes.
