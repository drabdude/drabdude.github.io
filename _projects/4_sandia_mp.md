Graduate research internship with Bobby Baraldi, Rapid Optimization Library team, Sandia National Laboratories, summer 2025.

PDE-constrained optimization problems are expensive because each iteration requires PDE solves and large linear algebra operations. I implemented mixed-precision gradient methods within trust-region algorithms for these problems and developed theoretical guarantees for them.
