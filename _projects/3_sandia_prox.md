Graduate research internship with Bobby Baraldi, Rapid Optimization Library team, Sandia National Laboratories, summer 2026.

Trust-region methods for nonsmooth, regularized optimization rely on evaluating proximal operators at every iteration. I developed viscosity-based approximations to these proximal operators for nonsmooth trust-region optimization.
